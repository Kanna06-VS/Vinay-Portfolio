What I do

Most ERP problems arrive dressed as a system error and turn out to be a process one. A posting group set up for a business that has since changed shape. A workflow nobody told the new joiner about. A report that was right in 2019. My job is to tell the difference quickly, fix the right layer, and write it down so the next person doesn't start from zero.

I work across the functional side of Business Central and NAV — finance, sales and purchase, inventory, warehouse, fixed assets — and increasingly the technical side too, because the handoff between the two is where most support time gets lost. I'd rather read the code and know than raise a ticket and wait.
